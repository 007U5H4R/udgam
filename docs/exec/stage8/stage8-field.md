# Stage 8 design critique: surface `field` (farmer/agent phone app)

Reviewer: `s8-field` (fresh design-reviewer). DES range used: DES-001 to DES-025.
Build under review: `build/stage7` head, `pnpm build` + `next start -p 4000`, `E2E=1`, fixture provider, throwaway auth secret, fresh `DATA_DIR`. Worlds seeded with `e2e/helpers/seed-capture.ts` (7 and 60 pickings, 2 plots, office phone, long farmer name); enrolment seeded with `scripts/seed-enrolment.ts`.
Viewports: 375×812 (primary), 360×740, 768×1024; reduced motion; Kannada (`udgam_lang=kn`). Not exercised: 1440 (field is a phone-first column; not in this surface's viewport list).
Mockup: `.design/exploration/final/index.html` rendered at the same viewports.
Evidence folder (all paths below are relative to it): `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-udgam/7d6abfcb-5350-51dc-9aa1-9a3d4f3c8522/scratchpad/stage8/field/`. Files named `_sheet-*.png` are contact sheets; `mock-*` are the mockup; `audit-*.json` hold the axe and measurement dumps.
Method note: the `impeccable` skill is not available; the stage-8 procedure was followed directly (running app + axe + manual measurement). Photos in picking detail are broken images only because the seed writes random bytes (seed artefact, not logged as a defect).

## Summary

| Severity | Count |
|---|---|
| P0 blocker | 0 |
| P1 major | 2 |
| P2 minor | 13 |
| P3 polish | 10 |

Overall: the field app is a faithful, high-quality port of the approved mockup. Record flow, checking, three verdicts, Pickings, states, reduced motion and contrast all hold up. The material problems are at the edges: the Help sheet's exit is off-screen on phones, and offline navigation drops the farmer onto the browser's error page.

## What matches the mockup / Design.md (confirmed with evidence)

- Visual language, tokens, radii, one primary pill per screen, lit word per screen, cherry cluster with green/amber rim and a bare cherry on Not accepted: `_sheet-verdicts.png` vs `_sheet-mock-b.png`.
- Record flow screens (photos, review, weight, checking with real ticking checks, verdicts) match mockups 1:1 at 375 and 360: `_sheet-rec-a.png`, `_sheet-rec-b.png`, `_sheet-p360.png`.
- Verdict words D5 ("Verified", "Needs a check", "Not accepted"); word + mark shape + colour on every chip. Forbidden-words scan of the farmer i18n keys is clean (no fraud/fake/cheat/suspicious/flagged/failed/rejected in farmer copy).
- Reduced motion: no animation anywhere on Home (`RM home animations []`), checking waits on "See result": `_sheet-rm.png`. Normal motion: only the slow pulse ring (`pulse 2.4s infinite`) on Home; cherry `rise` is on the verdict `<img>` (field.css 295).
- Contrast, measured from rendered pixels (not tokens): lowest text pair 6.33:1 (Not accepted chip), body ink 15.9:1, ink-2 8.2 to 9.9:1, disabled kg 6.41:1 at 82 px. EVAL-086 N8 (colour-only) holds for verdicts; see DES-007 for the two selection states that are colour-only.
- Focus: every control shows a 3 px `#D8F58C` ring; tab order = visual order (Language, Change plot, Record, Home, Pickings, Help).
- Layout: no horizontal scroll on any state at 375/360/768; `scrollWidth <= viewport` in all `audit-*.json` runs.
- Edge UX: a 63-character farmer name wraps cleanly on Home and in the plot chooser (`_sheet-edge-a.png`); a 60-picking history renders grouped by month in under 1 s (8,284 px page, no pagination; see DES-022); the end of Pickings clears the tab bar by 28 px.
- Axe (wcag2a/aa, 21, 22aa, best-practice) over 17 screens × 3 viewports + Kannada: zero critical, zero colour-contrast violations; findings listed in DES-014 and DES-020.
- Known items confirmed with my own evidence: QA-P6-8-5 tab bar (DES-012), underlined "What can I do?" (DES-016, matches the mockup), "used before" copy (DES-004). QA-P6-8-4, QA-M002-1, the og image, T1–T4 and EXE29/EXE32 copy are outside the field surface; nothing in the field app touches them.

## Deliberately not logged

- **Programmatic focus ring around the page `h1`** (yellow box on photos/review/checking in my shots): appears only when a screen is reached without a pointer interaction (my `page.goto`); after a tap the ring does not show. Correct behaviour for keyboard users.
- **Pre-ticked "Check:" rows on the photo review** ("It is in focus ✓"): they read as a passed check but are guidance; identical to the approved mockup. Personal preference, not logged.
- **Help sheet hosting verdict legend, language and phone id**: more than the mockup's two rows (Design.md §5 asks for them), the only issue is the overflow (DES-001).
- **"(demo data)" suffix** on fixture-derived lines (EXE12): deliberate and honest.
- **No gallery option**: Design.md §17 states the reason; Help explains it.
- **Dark UI in direct sunlight** (§22 risk): needs the owner's midday test; cannot be judged in a headless run. Contrast numbers are the best available proxy and are strong.
- **Mockup fonts**: the mockup loads Google Fonts, blocked offline here, so its screenshots use fallback faces; the app self-hosts Figtree/Noto Sans Kannada. I compared layout, not glyph shapes.
- **Verdict halo on Not accepted** (known): the bare cherry on Not accepted is correct for D5/TC-048 (no rim); the red tone sits on the badge only. No change recommended.
