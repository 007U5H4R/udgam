# Stage 8 design critique: public surface (DES-200..DES-299)

**Reviewer:** fresh design-reviewer subagent, clone `s8-public`, port 4040, `build/stage7` at the head used by the other reviewers.
**Surface:** the public certificate `/verify/[batchId]?h=`, its 404, `/` and `/sign-in`, the OG/link preview, the print view.
**Mockup:** `.design/exploration/final/verify.html`. **OG mockup:** `.design/exploration/og/verify.png` (+ `index.html`).
**How it ran:** `pnpm build`, `next start -p 4040` with `E2E=1`, a fresh `DATA_DIR`, a throwaway auth secret (never printed), `REMOTE_SENSING_PROVIDER=fixture`.
- Data: the Kodagu demo seed (`B-W4JKX2YY`, 5 pickings, 2 farms) plus my own seeded batches: plain 3-event, no organic, a 50-event/6-plot batch, an office override, a processor step within range, a flagged step, a processor-held batch with no step.
- Tools: Playwright at 375 / 768 / 1440, print emulation, `@axe-core/playwright`, DOM measurements, curl for the crawler-visible HTML.
- Evidence folder: `scratchpad/stage8/public/` (screenshots `*-{375,768,1440}-full.png`, mockup `mock-*`, scripts in `scripts/`). Paths below are relative to it.
- Servers stopped by PID; `.next`, the data dir and `node_modules` of the clone deleted. No tracked file edited.

## Summary

The certificate is solid where it matters most: the proof card is first at every width, Verified/mismatch/404/"could not check" all work and recover, no horizontal scroll at 375/768/1440, axe reports nothing in any state, there are no CSP violations, targets are all at least 48 px, the focus ring is visible and in visual order, and the mismatch state really has no green. A 50-event batch verifies in about 0.65 s.

The material problems are three: the origin map is close to unreadable and unlabelled (the demo batch's plots render as 10 px and 4 px specks, against the mockup's big labelled outlines); a batch held by a processor reads "Handed to buyer"; and the printed certificate has neither a QR nor a URL.

Severity counts: **P0 0 · P1 3 · P2 10 · P3 8** (21 findings, DES-200..DES-220).
