# Stage 8 design critique: surface `office` (admin, buyer, processor, M-002)

Reviewer: fresh design-reviewer subagent. DES range used: DES-100 to DES-114. Branch `build/stage7` @ 018b7c6, clone `s8-office`, built with `next build` and served with `next start` on port 4020 (`E2E=1`, `DEMO_MODE=1`, fixture satellite provider, a fresh data dir). The server was killed by PID, and `.next` and the data dir were deleted.

Data: `pnpm seed` (Kodagu demo: 12 plots, 30 pickings, 1 batch) plus the e2e seeds (`seed-agreements`: 7 agreements in every status; `seed-processing`; `seed-review`; `seed-batches`). Map tiles were stubbed (the Esri key is a placeholder), so the blue map fill in plot screens is an environment artefact and is not logged.

Evidence is in `scratchpad/stage8/office/`:
- Implementation shots: `a-*` (admin, `-1440/-768/-375`), `ag-*` (admin agreements), `b-*`/`bd-*`/`bt-*` (buyer), `p-*`/`pa-*` (processor), `act-*`/`dec-*`/`rr-*`/`demo-*` (flows and working/error states), `vp-*-375` (viewport shots), `t-*` (tall).
- Mockup shots: `mock/admin-*` and `mock/contract-*` at 1440, 768 and 375.
- Machine results: `results.jsonl`, one line per shot with axe violations, target sizes, horizontal scroll, h1 count and minimum font size.

Method: 5 flows were driven end to end (decide accept/not accepted; re-run; new agreement with a field check; fund; grade; settle; record step; hand on; issue and revoke a phone; upload a plot boundary). The four states were forced through `?state=` on every list and detail. 3 widths plus 320 were measured. An axe run (wcag2a/aa, 2.1 aa, 2.2 aa, best-practice) covered 252 renders. A keyboard tab-order walk, a forbidden-word scan and a long-name/no-space-name stress test were run.

### Deliberately not logged
- Dark glass and glow styling, rounded radii and the Opal direction: owner-chosen and frozen.
- The 12-check "All 12 checks" list showing `<code>` keys such as `signature_valid` and `quantity`: the mockups show them too (admin.html and contract.html); they are an auditor affordance.
- The admin Review "Failed" / "Failed · final" labels and "A check failed" sentence: they match `admin.html`. The M-002 accusation-word ban applies to agreements, steps and parties, and none of those screens uses the words (forbidden-word scan clean on every M-002 page).
- A score bar whose pointer sits in the "Needs a check" band while the verdict is Not accepted (hard-fail rule): the mockup does the same and the sentence explains it.
- The QR link `http://localhost:3000/…` on batch detail: caused by `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` being unset in this run (QA-P6-8-3 already requires an https base in production).
- The "Anvil (local test chain)" ledger line on a settled agreement: seeded e2e data; the production value is the real chain.
- A horizontal scroll that appeared in my first stress run: it came from injecting 90-character text into the rail and tab-bar labels, which is unrealistic. A realistic re-run (long and no-space names in the agreement rows, the plot rows and Kannada-script names) showed no horizontal scroll at 375; 320 px also had no scroll on 6 routes.
- Kannada strings: the office surfaces (admin, buyer, processor) are English-only by design; the 617 REVIEW strings belong to the field app. N/A here.
- Reduced motion: all runs used `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (the working states show the ring and words without movement). The default-motion run was not repeated for office surfaces, which carry no motion moment. Text at 200 % zoom was not run (see the done-gate table).
