    What this can’t prove

    These checks show that each picking was recorded inside a declared plot, with new photos, and that the records have not changed since they were sealed. They can’t prove what happened to the coffee after it left the FPO, and plot boundaries are declared by the farmers and compared with satellite maps — not surveyed on the ground. Weights are entered by the farmer, compared only with that plot’s usual harvest. Organic status comes from the certificate issuer, not from these checks.
